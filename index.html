About deep-shade

A place for photographs, moving images and the process behind them.

deep-shade combines street, portrait and brutalist studies with editorial notes and a lightweight photo and 4K video darkroom that runs directly in the browser. The visual system stays deliberately restrained: white space, black type, monochrome imagery and only the lines needed to give the content structure.
